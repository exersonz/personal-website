Modeling the Effect of Social Media Engagement in the Stock Market Using Dynamic Systems and Machine Learning

Aravind Arivudainambi · Cinty Lin · Michael Qu

Abstract

The stock market behavior mirrors the success of companies, yet its unpredictable fluctuation remains a challenge for accurate forecasting. Meanwhile, the recent rise of social media has become a huge indicator of success, driven by the emergence of online marketing campaigns and increased online presence. To simplify the chaotic nature of social media, we compress daily engagement metrics of companies into a Media Augmented Composite score (MAC). The evolution of MAC is modeled through a Media Augmented Composite Regression Operator (MACRO). We observed that MACRO heavily reflects companies' stock prices, as supported by our moderate correlation coefficient between the dynamic model and Apple's closing prices. After fitting a random forest network to MACRO, MACs, and stock data, MAC's mean decreased impurity (importance) in stock prediction increases with the testing window, confirming social media's significance in stock forecasting. Collectively, these models and metrics offer insights into quantifying, forecasting, and interpreting the influence of social media engagement on companies' financial performance.

Keywords: MAC, MACRO, social media, engagement metric, stock market prediction
