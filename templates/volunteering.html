Volunteering

Cyber Seniors

I'm proud to have been recognized as the June Volunteer of the Month for my work with Cyber Seniors! This organization helps seniors learn and navigate technology, bridging the digital divide and empowering older adults to connect with their families and communities.

Through one-on-one sessions and group workshops, I help seniors learn how to use smartphones, tablets, computers, and various apps. It's incredibly rewarding to see their confidence grow as they master new skills and discover the possibilities that technology offers.

CIP Origami Workshops

Leading origami workshops at CIP has been one of the most meaningful experiences in my volunteer work. What started as teaching a craft has evolved into deep, meaningful connections with the seniors I work with.

Even though I only see them a few times a month for about an hour each session, the relationships we've built are profound. The seniors feel comfortable opening up to me, sharing stories, and treating me like family. There's one senior in particular who I speak Mandarin and Taiwanese with, and she often shares personal stories with me, treating me like her granddaughter.

These connections remind me that meaningful relationships don't require constant presence—they're built on genuine care, respect, and the willingness to listen and connect across generations.
