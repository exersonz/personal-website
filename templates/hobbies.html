Hobbies

Crochet

Crocheting has become one of my favorite creative outlets. I love the process of transforming yarn into beautiful, functional pieces that I can use or give to others.

My Inspiration

Throughout my adolescence, I watched countless DIY videos that sparked my interest in crafting. I was particularly drawn to crochet because it allowed me to create cute, personalized items for myself and the people I care about. There's something special about making something with your own hands—it's both meditative and rewarding.

I also think crochet is a really cool craft, and it's something I want to continue doing throughout my life, especially when I'm older. It's a skill that brings joy, creativity, and a sense of accomplishment with every finished project.

Learning New Languages

Language learning has become a significant part of my life, and it all started with a moment of inspiration during a summer vacation.

How It Started

I was directly inspired by a close friend who was grinding away on Duolingo Korean while we were on vacation over the summer. Seeing their dedication and progress motivated me to start my own language learning journey. Additionally, my love for watching anime, listening to J-pop, and exploring Japanese culture made Japanese the natural choice for my first language to learn.

My Progress

Doing just a few lessons every day on Duolingo helped me build a solid foundation in Japanese. This preparation was so effective that I was able to skip Japanese 1 and jump directly into Japanese 2. Now I'm in AP Japanese and will be taking the AP Japanese test in May!

Future Goals

After college (or even during if feasible), I want to prepare and take the JLPT (Japanese Language Proficiency Test). I plan to work my way from N5 (or N4 if N5 is too easy) all the way to N1, which represents the ability to understand Japanese used in a variety of circumstances.

One of my biggest motivations is being able to fully enjoy Fujii Kaze's concerts if I ever get the chance to go! Understanding the language will make the experience so much more meaningful.

Other Languages I Want to Learn

Korean: I've recently gotten into K-pop, and I'd love to understand the lyrics and culture better.

German: I have several connections to German—an uncle who is proficient in German because he went to a German university for his doctorate degree, a family friend who lives there, and a close teacher friend who can speak German. These connections make learning German feel both practical and personally meaningful.

Through bullet journaling, I track my goals, document memories, and create a space for both productivity and personal reflection. It's become an essential part of my daily routine.
